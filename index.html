rclone-notes

A personal, single-user file sync utility.

What this application does

rclone-notes is a private, self-hosted tool used by its sole owner to keep a personal collection of plain-text notes synchronised between their own computer and their own Google Drive account. It runs locally using the open-source rclone utility and is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and is used by one person: the account holder.

What it accesses

With the Drive permission you grant, the tool reads and writes files in a single folder of your own Google Drive. That is its entire purpose.

Data handling

Files are copied directly between your computer and your Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any other service, no analytics are collected, and no data is shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party. The authorisation token is stored only on the owner's own machine.
